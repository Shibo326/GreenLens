EcoTech Corporation — Annual Sustainability Report 2025
=========================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

EcoTech Corporation is proud to announce that we have achieved carbon neutrality
across our global operations as of December 31, 2025. This landmark achievement
reflects our unwavering commitment to environmental stewardship and positions
EcoTech as an industry leader in sustainable technology manufacturing.

CARBON NEUTRALITY METHODOLOGY

Our carbon neutrality certification covers all Scope 1 (direct) emissions from
our manufacturing facilities, company-owned vehicles, and on-site energy generation.

Scope 1 Emissions Summary:
- Manufacturing facilities: 12,400 tCO2e
- Company fleet: 3,200 tCO2e
- On-site generators: 1,800 tCO2e
- TOTAL Scope 1: 17,400 tCO2e (fully offset)

Offsets purchased: 17,400 verified carbon credits from reforestation projects
in Southeast Asia (Gold Standard certified).

Note: Scope 2 (purchased electricity) and Scope 3 (supply chain, product use,
end-of-life) emissions are tracked but not included in our carbon neutrality
claim at this time. We are developing a roadmap to address these categories
by 2030.

Scope 2 Emissions (not included in neutrality claim): 89,000 tCO2e
Scope 3 Emissions (not included in neutrality claim): 342,000 tCO2e

Total actual footprint: 448,400 tCO2e
Percentage offset: 3.9% (Scope 1 only)

PACKAGING SUSTAINABILITY

100% of our retail packaging uses recycled materials. Our commitment to
sustainable packaging means every EcoTech product ships in boxes made from
post-consumer recycled cardboard.

Details:
- Outer shipping box: 100% post-consumer recycled cardboard
- Inner product tray: Virgin polystyrene foam (required for device protection)
- Plastic wrap: Standard LDPE film (not recyclable in most municipalities)
- Accessories packaging: PVC blister packs
- Total packaging weight: 340g per unit
- Recycled content by weight: 45% (outer box only = 153g of 340g)

WATER STEWARDSHIP

EcoTech has reduced water consumption by 15% year-over-year at our primary
manufacturing facility in Austin, Texas.

Note: This reduction was achieved by relocating water-intensive circuit board
washing processes to our new facility in Guadalajara, Mexico. Combined water
usage across both facilities increased by 8% year-over-year.

RENEWABLE ENERGY

We are committed to 100% renewable energy by 2028. Currently, 12% of our
total energy consumption comes from renewable sources (on-site solar panels
at our Austin headquarters). The remaining 88% is sourced from the regional
grid (ERCOT, Texas — approximately 23% renewable mix).

SUPPLY CHAIN

EcoTech sources components from 47 tier-1 suppliers across 12 countries.
Environmental audits are conducted on suppliers representing 30% of our
procurement spend. All major suppliers have signed our Supplier Code of
Conduct, which includes environmental commitments.

Supplier audit findings (2025):
- 14 of 47 suppliers audited (30%)
- 6 suppliers found non-compliant with wastewater standards
- 3 suppliers identified as using banned substances (later remediated)
- 33 suppliers not yet audited

E-WASTE AND CIRCULARITY

EcoTech offers a device trade-in program. In 2025, we collected 23,000
devices through this program. Of these:
- 8,000 refurbished and resold (35%)
- 4,000 harvested for parts (17%)
- 11,000 sent to certified e-waste recyclers (48%)

We are proud to report that zero EcoTech devices reached landfill through
our official channels in 2025.

CERTIFICATIONS
- ISO 14001 (Austin facility only)
- Gold Standard Carbon Credits (Scope 1 offsets)
- Energy Star (select product lines)
