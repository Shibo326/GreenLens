PRESS RELEASE — "EcoTech Achieves Carbon Neutrality"

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

EcoTech Corporation today announced it has achieved full carbon neutrality,
eliminating all greenhouse gas emissions associated with its operations.
The milestone makes EcoTech one of the first major consumer electronics
companies to reach net-zero emissions.

"This achievement represents years of hard work and investment," said
CEO Maria Chen. "Our customers can now purchase EcoTech products knowing
they come with absolutely no carbon footprint."

Key claims in this release:
- "Full carbon neutrality" — implies all scopes
- "Eliminating all greenhouse gas emissions" — implies zero actual emissions
- "Net-zero emissions" — conflates offsetting with elimination
- "Absolutely no carbon footprint" — unqualified absolute claim

WEBSITE FAQ — "Is EcoTech really carbon neutral?"

Q: Is EcoTech really carbon neutral?
A: Yes! We achieved carbon neutrality in 2025 by measuring and offsetting
   all emissions from our operations. We purchase Gold Standard certified
   carbon credits to ensure every ton of CO2 we produce is balanced out.

Q: What about your supply chain emissions?
A: We work closely with all our suppliers to reduce environmental impact.
   All suppliers sign our environmental code of conduct and undergo regular
   audits to ensure compliance with our standards.

Q: Is your packaging really 100% recycled?
A: Absolutely! We're proud that our packaging uses recycled materials,
   keeping waste out of landfills and reducing demand for virgin resources.
